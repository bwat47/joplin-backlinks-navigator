# Event brief

The workshop should help people leave with one useful skill and the confidence to try again at home.

## Scope

We can offer simple repairs, tool introductions, and advice about finding spare parts. We should avoid promising a repair for every item.

The [community workshop](Community%20workshop.md) is the main plan. A [clear meeting agenda](Planning%20agenda.md) will help us settle the remaining questions.

## Success

A welcoming room matters more than a large attendance number. Collect specific observations in the [feedback log](Feedback%20log.md).
