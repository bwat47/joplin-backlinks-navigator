# Venue notes

The neighborhood hall has a bright main room, a small kitchen, and folding tables stored behind the stage.

## Access

Use the side entrance for heavy boxes. The ramp is available from the courtyard; leave this route clear throughout the morning.

## Kitchen

Mugs are in the upper cupboard. Bring tea towels and take all opened food home afterward.

<a id="backup-contact">Backup contact</a>

If the caretaker is delayed, ask the volunteer at the adjacent library desk to contact the duty manager.

## Closing

Stack chairs in groups of six and leave the room ready for the afternoon booking. Confirm the final jobs against the [launch checklist](Launch%20checklist.md).
