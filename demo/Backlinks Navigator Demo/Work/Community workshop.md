# Community workshop

A friendly Saturday morning session for neighbors who want to repair small household items and share practical skills.

## Plan

We are aiming for twenty guests, three work tables, and enough spare time for people to talk. The [event brief](Event%20brief.md) keeps the scope manageable.

The [launch checklist](Launch%20checklist.md) brings the remaining tasks together. Review its [supplies](Launch%20checklist.md#supplies) before placing any orders, then confirm [setup](Launch%20checklist.md#room-setup) with the volunteers.

## People

The [volunteer rota](Volunteer%20rota.md) gives everyone one clear responsibility. Send the [invitation draft](Invitation%20draft.md) after the venue is confirmed.

## Practical details

Use the [budget](Workshop%20budget.md) to track shared costs and the [venue notes](Venue%20notes.md) for access information.

The [packing reminder](Launch%20checklist.md#supplies) is worth checking again on Friday. The [backup contact](Venue%20notes.md#backup-contact) can help if the main entrance is locked.

## After the event

Record what worked in the [feedback log](Feedback%20log.md). Compare the result with the [previous workshop](../Archive/Previous%20workshop.md) before planning another one.
