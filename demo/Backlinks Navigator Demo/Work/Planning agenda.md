# Planning agenda

A twenty-minute conversation to settle decisions before the workshop preparation begins.

## Discuss

1. Agree the [event brief](Event%20brief.md).
2. Check [venue access](Venue%20notes.md#access).
3. Assign the remaining [launch tasks](Launch%20checklist.md).
4. Confirm who will send the [invitation](Invitation%20draft.md).

## Finish

Read the decisions aloud and give every open action one owner.
