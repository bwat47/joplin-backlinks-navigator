# Launch checklist

Everything needed to turn the workshop plan into a calm Saturday morning.

## Before Friday

- [x] Confirm the [community workshop](Community%20workshop.md) scope.
- [ ] Send the final [invitation](Invitation%20draft.md).
- [ ] Ask each person to check the [volunteer rota](Volunteer%20rota.md).

## Supplies

Bring masking tape, spare pencils, extension leads, scrap paper, and a box for parts that need identifying. Keep the receipt with the [workshop budget](Workshop%20budget.md).

## Room setup

Put the welcome desk near the entrance and leave a wide route between tables. The [venue notes](Venue%20notes.md#access) explain which door to use.

## Final check

Open the [community workshop](Community%20workshop.md) plan before leaving home. Print a second copy of the [community workshop](Community%20workshop.md) outline for the welcome desk.

## Afterward

Add missing supplies to the [feedback log](Feedback%20log.md) while the details are fresh.
