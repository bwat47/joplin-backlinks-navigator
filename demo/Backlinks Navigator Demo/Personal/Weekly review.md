# Weekly review

A quiet half hour to close open loops and choose a manageable week.

## Clear the desk

Read [Home](../Home.md), sort loose notes, and check the [shopping list](Shopping%20list.md).

## Work

Look at the [community workshop](../Work/Community%20workshop.md), [website refresh](../Work/Website%20refresh.md), and [launch checklist](../Work/Launch%20checklist.md). Pick one useful next action for each.

Check the [design review](../Work/Review.md) before changing the website copy.

## Home

Make room for the [weekend walk](Weekend%20walk.md) and a little time in the [garden](Garden%20plan.md).

## Looking back

Use the [personal review](Review.md) to notice what helped rather than counting every unfinished task.
