# Garden plan

Make the small patio greener without turning watering into a second job.

## Early season

Start herbs indoors using the [seed starting](../Knowledge/Seed%20starting.md) notes. Reuse the pots already in the shed.

## Summer

Group thirsty plants together and leave room to sit outside. Add a few flowers that pollinators can reach easily.

## Notes for next year

Keep a record of what survived the hottest week and what needed more shade.
