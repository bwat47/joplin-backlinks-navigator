# Weekend walk

A gentle route along the river with time for a picnic and a stop at the old bridge.

## Route

Start at the station, follow the towpath north, and return through the park if the ground is dry.

## Pack

Use the [packing list](Packing%20list.md) and add lunch ingredients to the [shopping list](Shopping%20list.md).

## Weather

If rain is likely, shorten the route and spend the afternoon comparing notes from the [reading list](../Knowledge/Reading%20list.md).
