# Seed starting

A windowsill experiment with herbs and a few easy flowers.

## Setup

Use shallow trays with drainage, label each row, and keep a small watering jug nearby.

## Light & water

Check moisture before watering. Turn trays regularly so the seedlings do not lean toward the window.

## Next steps

Move strong seedlings into larger pots. Record timings in the [garden plan](../Personal/Garden%20plan.md) for next year.
