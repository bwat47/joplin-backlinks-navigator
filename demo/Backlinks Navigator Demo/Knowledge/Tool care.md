# Tool care

Shared tools last longer when the next person can find them clean, complete, and ready to use.

## After use

Wipe handles, return loose parts to their cases, and note anything missing. Add consumables to the [supplies list](../Work/Launch%20checklist.md#supplies).

## Storage

Store sharp edges with covers and keep damp items out of closed boxes. Review the [repair basics](Repair%20basics.md) before lending equipment to a new volunteer.
