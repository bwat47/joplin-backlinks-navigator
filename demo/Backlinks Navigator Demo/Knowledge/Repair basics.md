# Repair basics

Start by understanding the symptom, then make one small change at a time.

## Observe

Ask what happened before the fault appeared. Take a photo before removing any parts.

## Organize

Keep screws in a shallow tray and label anything that could be confused later. The [tool care](Tool%20care.md) notes explain how to leave shared equipment ready for the next person.

## Stop

If a repair needs specialist equipment or knowledge, help the owner identify a suitable repair service.
