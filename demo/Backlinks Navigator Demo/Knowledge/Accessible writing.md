# Accessible writing

Clear writing helps people act without having to guess what a sentence means.

## Headings

Use headings that describe the question the next paragraph answers. Prefer familiar words and keep instructions in the order people need them.

## Links

A link label should explain the destination even when read on its own. The [website refresh](../Work/Website%20refresh.md) is a useful place to practice this.

## Review

Read the text aloud once. Remove repeated explanations, and replace vague requests with a concrete next step.
