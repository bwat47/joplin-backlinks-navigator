# Meeting notes template

A light structure for conversations that need a clear outcome.

## Before

Write the decision needed and share the context early. The [planning agenda](../Work/Planning%20agenda.md) is a practical example.

## During

Separate observations, decisions, and actions. Put an owner beside each action.

## After

Send the result while everyone still remembers the discussion. Revisit open items during the [weekly review](../Personal/Weekly%20review.md).
